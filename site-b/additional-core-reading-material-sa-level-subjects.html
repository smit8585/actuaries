SA1 - Health and Care Specialist Application

The following reading material is relevant for SA1. The list below covers India specific legislations and regulations the candidate is expected to be aware of.

  • Master Circular on Actuarial, Finance and Investment Functions of Insurers 2024
  • Master Circular on Expenses of Management, including Commission, of Insurers, 2024
  • Master Circular on Reinsurance 2024
  • Master Circular on Operations and Allied Matters of Insurer 2024
  • Master Circular on Health Insurance Business 2024
  • Annexure to Master Circular on Health Insurance Business 29052024
  • Master Circular on Registration of Indian Insurance Company, 2023
  • IRDAI (Actuarial, Finance and Investment Functions of Insurers) Reg 2024
  • IRDAI (Expenses of Management, including Commission, of Insurers) Regulations, 2024
  • IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations, 2024
  • IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders’ Interests and Allied Matters of Insurers) Regulations, 2024
  • IRDAI Reinsurance Regulation, 2018 and (Amendment) 2023
  • IRDAI (Registration, capital structure, transfer of shares and amalgamation of insurers) Regulation ,2024
  • IRDAI (Registration and operations of Foreign Reinsurers Branches and Lloyd's India) Regulations, 2024
  • IRDAI (Rural, social sector and Motor third party obligations) Regulations, 2024
  • Master Circular on Corporate Governance for Insurers, 2024
  • IRDAI (Bima Sugam- Insurance Electronic Marketplace) Regulations, 2024
  • Reading Material for SA1

The above regulations may be amended by subsequent notifications.

Candidates are expected to keep themselves aware of latest regulations impacting the operations of health insurance segment, with particular focus on actuarial, product, investment & risk management, capital & solvency, reinsurance, and Policyholder protection.

Candidates may refer to http://www.irda.gov.in for latest changes and updates.
