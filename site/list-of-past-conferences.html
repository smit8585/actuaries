Virtual Actuarial Conclave (VAC)Actuaries Day

The GCA organized since 1999 is an annual signature event attracting stakeholders in the Insurance and financial sector, with an evening being devoted to Actuarial Gala Function and Awards (AGFA). The AGFA as well as GCA are organized with financial assistance from the Insurance and Pensions Industry, Consulting Organizations and other stakeholders in the event, called Partners.

Institute of Actuaries of India (IAI) organizes this event during the first quarter of the calendar year, where actuaries and non-actuaries assemble in a global ambience to share thoughts and debate matters that affect the financial services industry in general and insurance industry in particular. It is a great opportunity to witness the recognition of young actuarial professionals along with celebrations at Gala evening and learn/contribute papers/presentations on subjects that impact the industry at global level.

 It is a platform which brings renowned global speakers and eminent dignitaries from across the globe together and provides unmatched learning and networking opportunities to the members of actuarial community. It is one of the most sort after events amongst the students, who look forward to it every year to learn from the industry leaders and trend-setters and connect with the potential employers. With a new and emerging era of digitization, GCA is an effort to continue developing the profession and encouraging valuable discussions through its event platform.
