ABOUT THE PROGRAM

The world is secured with General Insurance products; be it Natural calamities, Vehicles, Property and Buildings, Health, Agriculture, Aviation, Marine, Cyber or any other tangible or intangible assets. The pricing of products is complex in nature due to various key influencing factors related to geography, society, culture and civilisation. Though the principles remain the same across all regions, methodology and assumptions are likely to vary significantly even within a society. To understand such complexity and variance of general insurance products, the lifetime of an individual may fall short. A comprehensive training on pricing of general insurance products might be appropriate and helpful to a larger group of actuarial members for their better, deeper and wider learning.

Who should attend this training and why?

a)  All unemployed actuarial students - You are going to fill the gap of trained actuarial resources required by many general insurance companies, not limited to India.
b) All employed actuarial students - this is a unique learning opportunity to broaden their area of expertise by interacting with expert faculty members.
c) All Associate and student members who are in employment - To change their career path by moving to General Insurance.
d) All Fellow members - To know dynamics of pricing of range of products under general insurance.

PROGRAM SCHEDULE :

The program scheduled under 13 sessions of 120 minutes each, starting from 10 Sep 2022 spread over 5 weeks. Participants are expected to work on assignments on a regular basis to maintain the continuity of learning and practice.

COVERAGES AND DURATION :

The program covers various techniques and approaches of pricing of general insurance products, viz., Motor, Health, Marine, Fire, Property, Personal Accident, Crop, Climate Risk and Emerging Products i.e. Cyber, Surety, Credit Insurance etc. along with underwriting and regulations aspects. Details in the program schedule below.

The training starts on 10 Sep 2022 and ends on 10 Oct 2022. All sessions conducted by GI actuaries who are prominent in the Industry.
