About the Institute

A statutory body for a profession built on trust.

Read more about IAI

The Institute of Actuaries of India is a statutory body established under The Actuaries Act, 2006 (35 of 2006) for the regulation of the profession of actuaries in India. The provisions of the Act came into force on 10 November 2006, by notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Finance, Department of Economic Affairs.
